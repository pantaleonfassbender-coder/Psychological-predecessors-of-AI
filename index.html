Rights. Every text this apparatus will carry is in the United States public domain (published through 1930); where no public-domain English translation exists, the site will supply its own working translation, marked as such. The corpus ends, deliberately, where the machines begin to learn: Hull's conditioning machine of 1929/30 is the last station — Craik, McCulloch & Pitts, Wiener and Turing remain in copyright, and are named in the coda.
